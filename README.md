## Selected projects

### [Olist Fulfilment BI](https://github.com/mzha9797-create/olist-fulfilment-bi)
**Python · SQL · SQLite · Power BI**

Built a reproducible analytics pipeline and dashboard to investigate delivery performance across regions, sellers and product categories.

- Analysed **99,441 orders**; among **96,470 eligible delivered orders**, the historical on-time rate was **93.23%**, with an order-weighted mean delivery duration of **12.56 days**.
- Designed a constrained relational database and order-level analytical views to avoid double-counting multi-item orders. Validated reconciliation, keys, constraints and restoration.
- Created an interactive Power BI report and documented a full-refresh workflow. The results support investigation; they do not demonstrate operational improvement or prove the cause of delays.

[Project and methodology](https://github.com/mzha9797-create/olist-fulfilment-bi) · [Written report](https://github.com/mzha9797-create/olist-fulfilment-bi/blob/main/Reports/Olist_Fulfilment_BI_Report.pdf) · [SQL views](https://github.com/mzha9797-create/olist-fulfilment-bi/blob/main/TechnicalPortfolio/01_Database/analytical_views.sql) · [Power BI snapshot](https://github.com/mzha9797-create/olist-fulfilment-bi/blob/main/TechnicalPortfolio/03_Dashboards/MBI807_Fulfilment.pbix)

### [New Zealand Business and Earthquake Analytics](https://github.com/mzha9797-create/nz-business-earthquake-analytics)
**Python · SQLite · Power BI**

Combined historical GeoNet earthquake records with Stats NZ business location data in an academic prototype for regional business-continuity screening.

- Processed **99,779 accepted earthquake events** from the 2020–2024 study area, retaining **17,290 offshore or unmapped events** visibly.
- Built regional and monthly aggregates, a constrained database and a three-page editable Power BI project. The analysis provides a starting point for investigation, not a prediction of earthquake risk or business losses.

[Explore the project](https://github.com/mzha9797-create/nz-business-earthquake-analytics)

### [SmartCare Aggregate Health Analytics](https://github.com/mzha9797-create/mbi806-smartcare-analytics)
**Python · Power BI · Data visualisation**

Explored a small academic smartwatch dataset using aggregate visualisation and a separate test of score regression.

- Compared a Ridge model with a training-mean baseline: test MAE was **13.99 versus 13.30** on a 10-record test set.
- Presented aggregate outputs while excluding sensitive row-level data. The model did not outperform the baseline and is not suitable evidence for automated clinical decisions.

[Explore the project](https://github.com/mzha9797-create/mbi806-smartcare-analytics)
